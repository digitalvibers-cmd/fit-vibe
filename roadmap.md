# Fit Vibe Product Roadmap

## Phase 1: MVP (Core Value) 
- [x] Landing Page & Registration (Gmail/Auth)
- [x] User Profile (Bio, Gender, Training Preferences)
- [x] Separate AI Generation for Workouts (Gym/Home, Equipment)
- [x] Separate AI Generation for Meals (Diet, Macros)
- [/] Tracking Progress (Weight logs, Goal definition)
- [ ] Basic Analytics (Summary on Dashboard)

## Phase 2: User Experience & Engagement
- [ ] **Gmail Calendar Integration**: Sync workouts to external calendar.
- [ ] **Daily Notifications**: Meal reminders and workout prompts.
- [ ] **Advanced AI Customization**: Substitution of exercises/meals.
- [ ] **Monetization**: Basic subscription structure.

## Phase 3: Gamification & Community
- [ ] **League System**: Competitive points based on activity.
- [ ] **Badges & Rewards**: Monthly winners and achievements.
- [ ] **Advanced Analytics**: BMI charts, calorie trends, and health insights.

## Future Enhancements
- [ ] Wearable device integration (Apple Watch, Garmin).
- [ ] AI Chat Support.
- [ ] Exercise video demonstrations.
